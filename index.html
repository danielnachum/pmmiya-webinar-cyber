With billions in funding, endless competitors, an ICP with zero patience for fluff, and disruptive AI, product marketing is one of the most critical functions a hyper-growth cybersecurity company needs.

Join a live webinar with Eyal Webber-Zvik, Chief Strategy Officer and formerly SVP of Product Marketing at Cato Networks, to talk about how to succeed as a product marketer in cybersecurity. Eyal managed the product marketing group for 8 years, helping to scale Cato from a startup to a category leader with $415M ARR, managing what 700 salespeople said on every call.

In this webinar we’ll talk about:

  • How the product marketing motion changes as you scale
  • How to find your category and lead it
  • How AI is reshaping product marketing in cybersecurity
  • Do CISOs want the technical details, or the bottom-line value?

You’ll walk away with insights on:

  • What product marketing in cybersecurity looks like in 2026
  • How to judge which work actually matters when everything feels urgent
  • How to do product marketing far from your market, and make it an advantage
